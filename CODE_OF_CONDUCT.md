# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of:
- age, body size, or visible or invisible disability
- ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression
- level of experience, education, or socio-economic status
- nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion
- sexual identity and orientation

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes:
- empathy and kindness
- respect for differing opinions
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- taking responsibility for mistakes
- focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- sexualized language or imagery
- trolling, insults or personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers privately. Use a [private security advisory report](https://github.com/adydeeram/SnapTable/security/advisories/new) marked "Code of Conduct", or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile.

All reports are reviewed promptly and in confidence. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is harmful, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
